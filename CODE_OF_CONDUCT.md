# Community conduct

Keep discussion respectful, specific and relevant to the project. Welcome questions from new users, explain technical disagreements, and criticize ideas rather than people.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, credential sharing and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when needed. Report concerns using the repository's available private reporting channel where enabled; do not repeat private information in a public issue. No dedicated response-time commitment is made for this volunteer project.
